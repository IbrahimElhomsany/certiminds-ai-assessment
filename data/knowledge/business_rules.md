# Ceramica Nova Official Business Rules

## BR-01 Outdoor slip resistance
Outdoor flooring should normally use slip-resistant products rated R11 or above.

## BR-02 Wall-only products
Wall-only ceramic products must not be recommended as flooring.

## BR-03 Commercial flooring
Commercial high-traffic flooring should prioritize porcelain products designed for commercial use.

## BR-04 Authoritative stock
Stock quantities are authoritative. The assistant must never fabricate inventory.

## BR-05 Wastage
A standard wastage recommendation is: simple layout 10%, diagonal or complex layout 15%.

## BR-06 Deterministic pricing
Price calculations must use deterministic software rather than LLM arithmetic.

## BR-07 Discount approval
No discount may exceed 12% without human approval.

## BR-08 Availability
Products below the required quantity (area in m2 for inventory purposes) should not be represented as immediately available.

## BR-09 Number of recommendations
Recommendations should normally contain no more than three primary products.

## BR-10 Missing information
If essential customer information is missing, the assistant should ask targeted questions instead of guessing.
