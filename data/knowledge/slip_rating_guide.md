# Slip Rating Guide (general industry guidance)

## What R ratings mean
R ratings (DIN 51130) describe slip resistance measured on a ramp test. A higher number means more slip resistance. R9 is the lowest rating and suits dry indoor areas. R10 suits normal indoor floors such as kitchens and living areas. R11 suits wet or outdoor areas and commercial spaces. R12 and above suits outdoor areas, pool surrounds, ramps and areas that are often wet.

## Finish and safety
Polished finishes are smooth and usually have lower slip resistance, so they suit dry indoor rooms. Matt and textured finishes provide more grip and are more appropriate for outdoor, wet or high-traffic areas.

## Porcelain versus ceramic
Porcelain tiles are denser and generally more durable, which suits floors and commercial use. Ceramic wall tiles in the Ceramica Nova catalog are intended for walls only.
